Beyond a wormhole near Saturn, three explorers have signaled from worlds that might give humanity a future. Cooper must choose between staying with his children and carrying the species toward a new home.

04THE FULL STORY

One mission.
One family.

Four chapters across the years. Expand each to trace the full arc.

On a blighted Earth, former NASA pilot Cooper and his daughter Murph discover a gravitational message leading to NASA’s hidden base. Professor Brand recruits Cooper to pilot the Endurance through a wormhole near Saturn. The mission has two possible plans: bring Earth’s population to a new home, or carry frozen embryos to seed one. Cooper promises Murph he will return.

The crew lands on Miller’s ocean world, dangerously close to Gargantua. A tidal wave kills Doyle and destroys the lander. The delay costs them 23 years aboard the Endurance. On Earth, Murph grows up, works with NASA, and learns Professor Brand’s equation cannot solve Plan A without data from inside a black hole. Brand had known this; Plan B was always his real plan.

With fuel for one destination, Cooper chooses the still-transmitting Dr. Mann over Edmunds’ silent world. Mann admits his planet is uninhabitable and that he faked the data to be rescued. He tries to take the Endurance, but a failed docking attempt kills him and damages the ship. Romilly is also killed by a booby-trapped robot. Cooper and Amelia Brand survive, with just enough fuel for a desperate maneuver.

Cooper and TARS fall into Gargantua so Brand can reach Edmunds’ planet. Inside, Cooper enters a tesseract where he can see Murph’s bedroom across time. He realizes he was the “ghost” behind the dust and watch messages. Using gravity, he sends TARS’s quantum data to adult Murph through the second hand of Cooper’s watch. Murph solves the equation, helping humanity leave Earth. The tesseract releases Cooper near Saturn, where he is rescued and reunited with an elderly Murph. He leaves to find Brand, who has reached Edmunds’ habitable world.
